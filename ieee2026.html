Q8Drone: A Benchmark Dataset for Tiny Object Detection in Aerial Traffic Scenes

Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-based sensing is becoming important for intelligent transportation systems because it provides flexible, wide-area traffic monitoring. However, this task faces challenges such as dense vehicle arrangements and background clutter, while high-altitude imaging makes vehicles small and hard to distinguish. Existing aerial datasets address some aspects of these challenges, but their geographic coverage remains limited. Regional differences in road layouts, traffic patterns, and weather may affect model generalization, including to Gulf environments. To address this gap, this paper introduces Q8Drone, a real-world UAV dataset collected in Kuwait for tiny vehicle detection. It contains 524 high-resolution RGB images of 7952 × 5304 pixels, providing wide-area views with 42,277 horizontal bounding-box annotations across five vehicle categories. Experiments evaluate CNN- and Transformer-based detectors in terms of class-wise performance, inference speed, and cross-generalization. Results reveal a domain gap when models trained on existing UAV datasets are evaluated on Q8Drone, supporting the value of regional datasets for intelligent transportation and smart-city IoT applications.
